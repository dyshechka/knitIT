Detailed Description

The VLineLengthLabel class text label placed at the middle of a straight line.

The label is scaled the same way as the point names are. It does not take mouse events itself, the view treats a click on it as a click on its line. The label can be dragged, the new offset from the middle of the line is reported to the owner of the label. A label that is far from the middle of its line gets a thin leader line to the middle, like a point name.
