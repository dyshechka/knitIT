36/**
37 * @brief The VLineLengthLabel class text label placed at the middle of a straight line.
38 *
39 * The label is scaled the same way as the point names are. It does not take mouse events itself,
40 * the view treats a click on it as a click on its line.
41 * The label can be dragged, the new offset from the middle of the line is reported to the owner of the label.
42 * A label that is far from the middle of its line gets a thin leader line to the middle, like a point name.
43 */
